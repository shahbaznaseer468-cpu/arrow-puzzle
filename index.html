Privacy Policy

Arrow Puzzle • Effective Date: October 9, 2026

Summary: Arrow Puzzle is designed with your privacy in mind. We do NOT collect, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). All your game progress, stars, and customized levels are stored securely and locally on your own device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Puzzle. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data when you download, install, and play Arrow Puzzle on your Android mobile device.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Arrow Puzzle operates as an offline-first puzzle game:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for, collect, or store personal details such as your name, physical address, phone number, contacts, location, or biometric data.
  • Local Device Storage: Game progress, unlocked levels, scores, sound/music preferences, and levels designed in the Custom Level Editor are saved locally on your device via standard Android device storage (Shared Preferences). This data never leaves your device.

3. Permissions Requested

Arrow Puzzle requires minimal device permissions solely necessary for core game mechanics:

  • Vibration (Haptics): Used exclusively to deliver tactile haptic feedback when tapping arrows or colliding with obstacles (can be disabled in Settings at any time).
  • Audio Output: Used exclusively to play local in-game sound effects and background melodies.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

We do not track your activity across other apps or websites. Standard Google Play services may process basic non-identifying telemetry (such as crash diagnostic reports and app installation statistics) in accordance with Google's Play Services Privacy Policy to ensure the stability of the game.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Puzzle is a family-friendly logic puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly ensure it is removed.

6. Security of Your Data

Because your game data resides strictly on your local device, the security of your game progress is safeguarded by your device's native operating system security features.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy occasionally.
